New in my Copilot repo: Microsoft Copilot Custom Personalization Instructions.

How to set Copilot custom instructions, with examples for different roles (Copilot admin, identity admin, students, HOA board member).

Instead of repeating those preferences in every prompt, Copilot can use them as context for future interactions.

The result is usually much closer to what you would have written yourself.

What you get: Better answers without repeating your role and preferences in every prompt
