New in my Copilot repo: Copilot Agent Delegation Guide.

The Agent Delegation Guide: who can build, share, publish and receive agents, using the groups from step 1.

This guide explains who can build Copilot agents, who can share and publish them, and how to give agents to people in Microsoft 365.

Microsoft changes these settings often. Check each path in your own tenant before you rely on it.

You'll need:
• Microsoft 365 Copilot with Copilot Cowork

What you get: Agent creation and sharing limited to the right people
